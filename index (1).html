About 3G Flora

At 3G Flora, we specialize in high-end botanical resin art across three distinct verticals: preserving priceless floral memories, crafting unique resin home decor, and creating memorable return gifts. We turn fleeting moments into timeless, handcrafted pieces that bring beauty and sentiment into everyday life.

What we do

Floral Memory Preservation (Keepsakes)

Professional preservation of real flowers from life’s major milestones—such as wedding varmalas, anniversary bouquets, and special event blooms.

Key products: Custom varmala display frames, resin preservation blocks, and floral keepsake plaques that keep sentimental memories alive permanently.

Bespoke Resin Art & Home Decor

Designing one-of-a-kind, aesthetic resin creations to elevate living and working spaces.

Key products: Custom designer nameplates, resin wall art, decorative thalis, diya holders, and statement accent pieces made with real botanicals, gold leaf accents, and fine detailing.

Curated Return Gifting

Bulk and customized gifting solutions for weddings, housewarmings, corporate events, and festive celebrations.

Key products: Personalized resin keychains, pressed flower bookmarks, mini trinket dishes, custom tea coasters, and tailored gift hampers designed to leave a lasting impression on your guests.

How we can help you

Event planners & wedding clients

3G Flora provides end-to-end floral and resin art services for your big events—from preserving the bride and groom’s wedding varmalas into lasting keepsake frames, to designing handcrafted return gifts for your guests.

Home decor & retail inquiries

3G Flora transforms natural botanicals into modern art. Whether you are looking for custom nameplates and home decor items or want to preserve a special bouquet, we craft unique, handmade resin pieces tailored to your style.
